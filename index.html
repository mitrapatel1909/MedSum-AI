Cancer immunotherapy · checkpoint inhibitors Landmark RCTs · outcomes analysis

PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint inhibitors (pembrolizumab, nivolumab) have shown durable responses across multiple solid tumor types. In advanced melanoma, 5-year overall survival reached 34% with pembrolizumab monotherapy in KEYNOTE-001 and 52% with nivolumab plus ipilimumab in CheckMate 067 — each figure reflects a distinct treatment group and trial design. Exploratory analyses suggest associations between response rates and biomarkers such as tumor mutational burden and PD-L1 expression; their role in guiding treatment decisions is an active area of research.

Sources: PMID 30715153 (KEYNOTE-001, 5-yr OS) · PMID 31562797 (CheckMate 067, 5-yr OS) · PMID 34389558 (exploratory biomarker analysis)
